\section{What the app does}

Interactive companion to Deliverable~2. It lets you (i) inspect each of the
twelve priors over the unknown cell $Z$, (ii) inspect the four detection
models $\rho_j(d,r,e)$, (iii) preview what each of the four search
strategies would propose given the current prior and detector, (iv) run a
full interactive search campaign against a planted target, and (v) view the
final operational pipeline applied end-to-end. All maps are computed
live; no MCMC is required at runtime (Laplace / cell-level update only).

\section{Sidebar --- global configuration}

Four controls live in the left sidebar and apply across all tabs:

\smallskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
  \textit{Prior}
    & one of the twelve registered priors P3--P14. Default is the
      operational pick \texttt{P13\char`_impact\char`_robust\char`_uniform10}. \\
  \textit{Detector}
    & one of the four detector families D1--D4. Default is
      \texttt{D1\char`_saturating\char`_exponential}. \\
  \textit{Update temperature}
    & $T \ge 1$ in $\pi_{\mathrm{post}}(j) \propto \pi_{\mathrm{prior}}(j)\,L_j^T$.
      $T=1$ is the textbook Bayes rule; $T=1.5$ amplifies the
      partial-failure signal (operational default). \\
  \textit{Budget cap}
    & total cost allowed across the campaign (max $530$). \\
\end{tabularx}

\section{The six tabs}

\textit{1. Overview.} Plots the accident point $x_E$, the velocity vectors
$(v_{\mathrm{plane}}, v_{\mathrm{wind}}, v_{\mathrm{drift}})$, the expected
impact $\mu(\tau_{\mathrm{fall}}, \tau_{\mathrm{drift}})$ and the
trajectory axes $(\hat d, \hat n)$. Useful to build intuition before
playing with priors.

\textit{2. Priors.} Heatmap of the selected prior plus a $4{\times}3$ grid
of all twelve priors at the same colour scale --- the place to compare how
much each prior concentrates around $\mu$ versus spreading laterally.

\textit{3. Detectors.} Heatmap of $\rho_j$ for the selected detector at
the chosen effort $e\in\{1,2,3\}$, plus the four detector families side by
side at the same effort.

\textit{4. Strategy preview.} For the current (prior, detector) it shows
what each of the four strategies (\textsc{fixed}, \textsc{adaptive},
\textsc{info\_gain}, \textsc{progressive scenario search}) would propose
as the next rectangle and effort. No mission is executed.

\textit{5. Simulator.} The main interactive page. Plant a target (manual
cell or random), then either fire a mission manually
(rectangle + effort) or let the selected strategy propose one and execute.
The outcome $s_t\in\{0,1\}$ is drawn from the true detector, the cell-level
posterior is updated with temperature $T$, the heatmap refreshes, and the
mission is appended to the on-screen history table. Budget and entropy
counters update live.

\textit{6. Operational model.} The final delivered pipeline executed
end-to-end with no user input: prior \texttt{P13}, detector
\texttt{D1\char`_saturating\char`_exponential}, strategy
\textit{progressive scenario search} with the \texttt{mixture} hypothesis,
budget $B=530$. Shows the full mission sequence under repeated
non-detection (the \texttt{missions.csv} export), the final posterior, and
the per-mission posterior evolution.

\section{Typical walkthroughs}

\textit{To compare priors.} Tab~2, eyeball the $4{\times}3$ grid, switch
the sidebar prior to see the selected one larger.

\textit{To compare strategies on a single state.} Tab~4 with a fixed
sidebar (prior, detector).

\textit{To run a complete campaign by hand.} Tab~5, plant a target, pick
the strategy you want from the dropdown inside the tab, click \emph{run
mission} repeatedly until the budget is exhausted or the target is found.

\textit{To reproduce the deliverable.} Tab~6, no clicks needed --- it
re-executes the same configuration that produced \texttt{missions.csv}.

\section{Reset and caching}

All priors and detector maps are cached (\texttt{st.cache\char`_data}). If
you change the sidebar values the affected tabs recompute; the others reuse
the cache. To clear simulator state (planted target, mission history),
press \emph{Reset} at the top of tab~5.
